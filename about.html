Who We Are

Crafted with Purpose,
Designed for Life

Zivya Enterprises represents a refined approach to handcrafted design, where heritage craftsmanship meets modern sophistication. Based in Moradabad, India — the craft capital of the world — we have been creating premium lighting and décor pieces for over a decade.

Every piece that leaves our workshop is a testament to the skill of our artisans, the quality of our materials, and the precision of our finishing — crafted to meet the standards of global markets and the expectations of discerning clients.
